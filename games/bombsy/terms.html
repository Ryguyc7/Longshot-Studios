The rules of play.

1. Using Bombsy

These terms describe use of Bombsy and its puzzles, rewards, and customization features. You may play for your own personal entertainment. If you are not old enough to agree to terms where you live, a parent or guardian must review them and permit your use.

Follow the laws that apply to you and any applicable rules of the browser, device platform, or app store through which you access the game.

2. Your local player profile

No email or password is required to play. The game automatically creates an anonymous online session on this device. A display name and randomly generated player ID belong to the save on this device. Names are not exclusive, and matching names do not mean two profiles are the same person.

Display names are limited to 24 characters and must be suitable for other players. Do not use hateful, sexually explicit, threatening, or abusive names, impersonate someone else, or include another person’s private information. Name filtering may reject a name that does not meet these rules.

3. Fair play and game content

Do not use the game to harass others, distribute harmful software, interfere with game services, or bypass safeguards to obtain unauthorized rewards or access. Do not modify the app or submit fabricated puzzle results to manipulate rankings.

Game artwork, music, branding, and other content remain the property of their respective owners. Playing the game does not give you ownership of that content or permission to sell or redistribute it. Rights granted by applicable law are not limited by this notice.

4. Stars, gifts, and unlocks

Stars, gifts, tools, and cosmetics are game features with no cash value. They cannot be cashed out, transferred between profiles, traded, or exchanged for real-world goods through Bombsy.

A completed puzzle can earn up to 3 stars: 3 for keeping every heart and using no hints, reveals, or shovels; 2 for finishing with at least 2 hearts otherwise; and 1 for finishing with 1 heart. Improving a previous result awards only the increase over your best rating.

A gift costs 10 earned stars and opens immediately. Each gift unlocks one randomly selected item you do not already own. There are no duplicate unlocks, and the shop stops selling gifts when your existing gifts cover the remaining collection. Stars do not directly unlock customization items.

Every 7 consecutive daily puzzle completions earns a separate gift plus 5 bonus stars. Rewards, prices, available items, and puzzle rules can change in later versions; the game will show the rules and price that apply before you spend stars.

5. Tools and optional advertising

A new profile starts with 5 hints, 5 reveals, and 5 shovels. Tools are consumed when used according to their game rules. They are not guaranteed to carry across devices or deleted saves.

No live ad provider is enabled in this version. Each tool regenerates one use every 25 minutes while below 5 uses, including time spent offline or with the app closed. Timers pause when that tool is full; extra uses already granted by gifts are retained. Available tools can be used offline.

If a supported ad service is added in a future version, you may be offered a rewarded video for one extra use of the selected tool. The extra use would be granted only when the service confirms the reward. Cancelling or failing to complete a video would not grant it.

6. Real-money payments

This version does not sell stars, gifts, tools, or items for real money. Buying a gift means spending stars earned in the game. If real-money purchases are introduced, their price, payment provider, and applicable purchase or refund terms will be disclosed before purchase.

7. Saved progress and account deletion

Progress is stored locally, with no automatic cloud backup of your complete save or cross-device transfer. Clearing browser data, uninstalling an app, device changes, or storage problems can remove progress, unlocks and signed ranking recovery records.

Anonymous online accounts can be cleaned up after 30 days without online activity. Scored accounts are protected until the app confirms that all current server-signed recovery records are saved locally. When you reconnect with those records after cleanup, the game creates a new online identity and restores verified Global scores. Weekly credits and Daily results retain their original dates; restoring old results does not award stars again. Your locally saved name, progress and items remain on the same device. Recovery is unavailable if the records have been erased or damaged.

Settings → Privacy → Delete Account permanently removes your online identity, leaderboard profile and ranking results, clears and invalidates ranking recovery records, discards pending uploads and stops future uploads. An internet connection is required if an online account exists. Your local progress and unlocks remain on this device. You can explicitly join the leaderboard again to create a new online account for future results; deliberately deleted ranking results cannot be restored.
